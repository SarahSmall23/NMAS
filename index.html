How it works

Built around the way a hospital really runs

Every facility works with its own patients, staff and pharmacy stock, while a patient's history stays reachable if they are treated somewhere else.

Check-in at reception

Walk-ins are registered at the front desk. Booked follow-ups are recognised and go ahead of the queue.

Vitals before the doctor

Nurses capture blood pressure, temperature, glucose and more, then send the patient to the right doctor.

Consult and prescribe

Doctors see the full history, write structured prescriptions and book the follow-up before the patient leaves.

Features

Everything a facility needs, in one place

From the front desk to the pharmacy shelf to the facility manager's report.

Shared patient history

Consultations, prescriptions and visits follow the patient across facilities. Every cross-facility view is logged and the home facility is notified.

Smart prescriptions

Choose from the facility's real stock, with dose guidance and a validity period so expired prescriptions can't be dispensed.

Pharmacy per facility

Each pharmacy manages its own stock, expiry dates and low-stock alerts, and can see whether a medicine was already given elsewhere.

Video consultations

Patients request a call during the doctor's published schedule, with prescriptions sent straight from the call.

Follow-up bookings

Doctors book check-ups and the patient receives a notification, an email and a PDF slip to confirm.

Performance reports

Waiting times, workload, stock health, portal activity and an automatic list of problems to fix early, as PDF or spreadsheet.

Who it's for

A tailored workspace for every role

One login page. After signing in, each person lands on their own dashboard with only what they need.

Patients

View your profile, prescriptions, appointments and who accessed your records. Talk to AIDEN or request a video call.

Receptionists

Register patients and check in walk-ins and booked follow-ups. Reception does not issue or manage repeat-pill collection windows; it only confirms a patient has arrived for a collection the pharmacy already has due.

Nurses

Work the vitals queue and pass patients to doctors with their readings attached.

Doctors

Your own queue, full histories, prescriptions, video consultations and follow-up bookings.

Pharmacists

Dispense from your own stock, track expiry, and download stock reports.

Facility administrators

Register your staff, see your facility's audit trail and cross-facility access, and run operations reports.

System administration

Register facilities and administrators, and see any facility's performance from one portal.

Everyone

Role-based access, audit logging and notifications keep every action accountable.

AIDEN symptom assistant

Tell AIDEN how you feel, in your own language

AIDEN listens to what you actually say, answers your questions, asks only what it still needs to know, and works out how urgent your situation is. If you need an ambulance it tells you so straight away. It suggests a doctor only when a consultation is really needed.

  • Understands and replies in the South African languages: English, Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sepedi, Setswana, Sesotho, Xitsonga, siSwati, Tshivenda and isiNdebele.
  • Talk to AIDEN like a voice note: speak, pause, and it answers. No timers or settings.
  • Optionally uses your own NMAS history, only if you switch it on.
  • AIDEN is an AI helper, not a doctor, and never replaces a medical visit.
