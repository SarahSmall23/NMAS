📦 Storage-Based Dispensing Policy: Medication is dispensed exactly as it's kept in the dispensary — whole packs for tablets/capsules, and whole vials, tubes, bottles, inhalers or patches for insulin, creams, syrups, inhalers and the like. For pack-tracked items the system calculates the number of packs required from the prescribed dosage and duration; everything else is dispensed as a whole container, matching how it's stored.
